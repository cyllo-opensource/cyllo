Cyllo Commission
----------------

The Cyllo <a href="https://www.cyllo.com/commission">Commission</a> app helps you create
and manage commission plans for sales teams and individual salespeople to boost
their productivity. It computes earnings automatically from paid sales orders and
won opportunities, and presents the results through detailed reports and an
interactive dashboard.

Target And Contribution Plans
-----------------------------

Build commission plans of two kinds: target plans that reward salespeople once
they reach configurable achievement rates against a period goal, and contribution
plans that pay a percentage rate on the value of qualifying sales or leads.
Optional bonus tiers reward over-achievement beyond the on-target commission.

Team And Salesperson Assignment
-------------------------------

Assign a plan to a whole sales team or to specific salespeople, with validation
that prevents the same user or team from being covered by overlapping plans of
the same type. Approval and rejection workflow states, plus duplicate-user
detection, keep commission assignments clean and unambiguous.

Flexible Commission Periods
---------------------------

Choose a monthly, quarterly, or yearly frequency and Cyllo automatically builds
the matching periods between the plan's start and end dates. Commissions are
calculated per period so payouts always align with the timeframe each salesperson
worked within.

Rule-Driven Commission Types
----------------------------

Define reusable commission types based on either sales orders or CRM leads, each
with its own domain rule to filter exactly which records qualify. Only paid orders
(and their related won opportunities) are considered, ensuring commissions reflect
real, collected revenue.

Interactive Dashboard And Reports
---------------------------------

A dedicated dashboard aggregates plans, commissions, orders, salespeople, teams,
and won opportunities with summary cards, performance graphs, a leaderboard, and
commission-distribution views. Salespeople can review their own commissions while
managers access full commission and contribution reports.

Automated Notifications And Excel Export
----------------------------------------

Approving a plan sends welcome and duplicate-plan notification emails to the
affected salespeople using built-in mail templates, and a scheduled action closes
plans once their end date passes. Commission data can also be exported to a richly
formatted XLSX report complete with an executive summary and top-performer
leaderboard.
