Cyllo Quality
-------------

The Cyllo <a href="https://www.cyllo.com/quality">Quality</a> app helps you enforce
quality standards across your inventory operations by defining control points,
generating quality checks on transfers, and tracking issues through quality alerts.

Quality Control Points
-----------------------

Configure control points that target specific operation types and either products
or product categories, and choose how they are triggered - for every operation,
randomly, or periodically over a defined frequency and period.

Automatic Checks on Transfers
-----------------------------

When a transfer is confirmed, matching control points are detected automatically and
quality checks are generated per operation, product, or a percentage-based quantity,
and a transfer cannot be validated until its required checks are completed.

Guided Inspection Actions
-------------------------

Each control point holds ordered inspection actions typed as pass/fail or measure,
with measure ranges, units, blocking dependencies, and custom instructions that guide
operators line by line during a check.

Pass and Fail Tracking
----------------------

Quality checks move through To Do, Ongoing, Pass, and Fail states computed from their
individual check lines, so a single failed line marks the whole check as failed.

Quality Alerts
--------------

Raise alerts linked to a check, product, lot, or transfer, and manage them across
kanban stages such as quarantine while recording descriptions, corrective actions,
preventive actions, and priorities.

Quality Teams and Notifications
-------------------------------

Organise inspectors into quality teams led by an employee, and send automatic email
notifications to the team lead whenever a new control point is created.
