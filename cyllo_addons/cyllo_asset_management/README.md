Cyllo Assets Management
-----------------------

The Cyllo <a href="https://www.cyllo.com/assets-management">Assets Management</a> app extends the
accounting module to cover the complete lifecycle of company assets, from registration and
depreciation through reservation, assignment, leasing, maintenance and final disposal.

Asset Registration & Categorization
-----------------------------------

Register assets with their brand, serial number, purchase date, vendor and company, and group them
under asset items that carry the shared depreciation and accounting configuration. A status field
tracks each asset through its lifecycle states.

Depreciation & Accounting Integration
--------------------------------------

Compute depreciation using configurable methods, factors, durations and periods, generating
depreciation lines and posted journal entries against the dedicated asset and depreciation accounts.
A scheduled action drives the recurring depreciation postings.

Asset Lifecycle Operations
--------------------------

Reserve assets for a period, then assign them to employees or departments, or lease and rent them to
customers with amounts, payment terms and invoicing. Actions guide each asset through reservation,
assignment, lease, rental and return, keeping its status in sync.

Booking & Availability Conflicts
--------------------------------

A central booking model records every period an asset is committed and checks for overlaps, including
a configurable buffer, so the same asset cannot be double-booked across reservations, leases, rentals
and maintenance. Availability warnings surface conflicts before they are confirmed.

Maintenance, Warranty & Insurance
---------------------------------

Raise maintenance and repair requests linked to assets, tracking expenses, repair partners and scrap.
The app checks warranty and insurance coverage, supports insurance reimbursement, and manages
insurance policies by provider and insurance type.

Sell & Dispose
--------------

Retire assets by selling or disposing of them, posting the appropriate journal entries and recording
gains or losses through the configured loss account. Disposal captures the remaining depreciated
amount and disposal type for a clean accounting close-out.
