Cyllo HRMS
----------

The Cyllo <a href="https://www.cyllo.com/hrms">HRMS</a> app simplifies employee management
with a complete suite for attendance tracking, payroll processing, performance evaluation,
and recruitment, all seamlessly integrated into one platform.

Automate the entire employee lifecycle and unify your HR operations, from hiring and
onboarding to payroll and appraisals, in a single connected system.

Manage Talent with Confidence
-----------------------------

Automate the complete employee lifecycle by centralizing recruitment, digital onboarding,
and attendance in one dashboard. Integrated tools for payroll automation, appraisals, and
expense management work alongside staff self-service for leave and document management.

Smarter Workforce Management
----------------------------

Unify your HR operations by connecting recruitment, attendance, timesheets, and employee
contracts into a single experience, giving you enhanced control and greater efficiency
across every department.

One Platform for Your Entire Workforce
--------------------------------------

Consolidate hiring, onboarding, time tracking, and employee records in one connected
system, so your organization stays streamlined and every piece of information is exactly
where you expect it.

Hire Smarter, Not Harder
------------------------

Manage job postings, applications, and interviews from one organized space. Publish
openings directly to your website with a single click and keep the full hiring journey
moving without the paperwork.

Manage Contracts with Clarity and Control
-----------------------------------------

Create, track, and organize employee agreements centrally. Monitor contract details,
durations, and statuses so every commitment stays visible and up to date.

Track Attendance Without the Hassle
-----------------------------------

Monitor real-time check-ins and check-outs, with kiosk mode for instant clocking in.
Turn accurate time entries into clear insights on productivity across tasks and projects.
