Cyllo CRM
---------

The Cyllo <a href="https://www.cyllo.com/crm">CRM</a> app is an optimized customer
relationship platform built for rapid business expansion, turning potential leads into
loyal clients from one centralized, actionable view.

Consolidate every customer relationship in a single place with automated follow-ups and
real-time tracking that keep your team close to every opportunity.

Turn Lead Data Into Momentum
----------------------------

Dynamic dashboards transform revenue, leads, and pipeline metrics into forward motion for
your sales team. Convert insights into action by prioritizing high-value leads and
accelerating movement through every stage of the pipeline.

Every Lead. Every Stage. One View.
----------------------------------

A visual Kanban pipeline displays all your leads by stage, letting you create, assign, and
move opportunities across the sales process in an instant. Add and activate a new lead
on the spot and route it through your pipeline immediately.

Capture More. Convert Smarter.
------------------------------

Generate leads from multiple channels including emails, live chats, and website messages.
Contact form submissions are automatically captured as leads for immediate follow-up and
team assignment, so no opportunity slips away.

Stage-Wise Insights, Visualized
-------------------------------

Stacked bar charts reveal workload balance, movement trends, and overall pipeline health
in real time. Multiple visualization options help your team spot stage imbalances and make
faster, conversion-focused decisions.

Everything You Need to Grow
---------------------------

With recurring revenue tracking, predictive lead scoring, multichannel lead creation, deal
management, meeting integrations, and lead capture through surveys, Cyllo CRM equips you to
build a superb strategy for every customer.
