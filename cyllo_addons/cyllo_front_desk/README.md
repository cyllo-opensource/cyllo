Cyllo Front Desk
----------------

The Cyllo <a href="https://www.cyllo.com/front-desk">Front Desk</a> app manages your
reception operations end to end, letting you run multiple front desk stations, register
and track visitors, notify hosts automatically, and handle enquiries, refreshments and
emergency situations from a single place.

Multiple Reception Stations
----------------------------

Set up as many front desk stations as your premises need, each with its own responsible
employees, and toggle per-station options such as host selection, drink offerings and the
notification channels (Email, SMS or Discuss) used to reach hosts.

Visitor Check-In and Check-Out
------------------------------

Register visitors with an auto-generated reference and move them through a clear lifecycle
of Draft, Planned, Checked In, Checked Out and Cancelled. Check-in and check-out times are
recorded automatically and the visit duration is computed for you.

Meeting Approval Workflow
-------------------------

For meeting-type visits, send the host an approval request email containing secure Approve
and Reject links backed by a unique access token, so hosts can accept or decline expected
visitors directly from their inbox before arrival.

Automatic Host Notifications
----------------------------

When a visitor checks in, the assigned host, the station's responsible employees and any
drink preparation team are notified through Email and Discuss according to each station's
configuration, keeping everyone informed in real time.

Enquiry Management
------------------

Log walk-in enquiries with type, subject, priority and handling staff, then track them
through New, In Progress, Closed and Lost stages. Each enquiry automatically creates a
linked visitor record and can be converted into a matched or newly created contact.

Refreshment Requests
--------------------

Maintain a catalogue of drinks with images and sequencing, let visitors request a drink
during registration, notify the responsible staff, and mark drinks as served once
delivered.

Emergency Alerts
----------------

Configure reusable emergency alerts tied to specific stations, with a default message and
recipients spanning employees, users and Discuss channels, and trigger them instantly from
the station through a dedicated wizard.
