Cyllo Marketing Automation
--------------------------

The Cyllo <a href="https://www.cyllo.com/marketing-automation">Marketing Automation</a> app powers your marketing
by automating workflows, managing drip campaigns, scoring leads, triggering actions and engaging customers across
every channel.

Marketing That Works 24/7
-------------------------

Turn manual outreach into a multi-channel engine that never sleeps. Cyllo delivers personalized email and SMS
messages in response to real customer behavior, driving growth around the clock.

Automate the Moment a Lead Is Born
----------------------------------

Set up smart automation rules that target specific contacts, assign ownership and trigger the right actions
automatically. Rule-driven workflows make sure no new lead ever slips through the cracks.

Turn Every Interaction Into Opportunity
---------------------------------------

Automated follow-ups, reminders and activity tracking keep leads engaged and moving steadily toward conversion,
so every interaction becomes a chance to close.

Craft Campaign Journeys That Flow Like a Story
----------------------------------------------

Build dynamic campaign paths where emails and follow-ups unfold in sequence. Design each journey visually and
launch the whole experience with a single click.

Track Every Participant, Every Step
-----------------------------------

See the real-time status and progress of every campaign participant, including engagement metrics and exactly
where each contact sits within your workflow.

Advanced Campaign Toolkit
-------------------------

Automated campaigns, conditional triggers and a built-in test mode give you everything you need to design complex
marketing workflows with ease and free up your team's resources.
