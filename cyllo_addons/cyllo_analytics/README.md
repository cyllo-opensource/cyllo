Cyllo Analytics
---------------

The Cyllo <a href="https://www.cyllo.com/cyllo-analytics">Analytics</a> app delivers AI-driven insights
for a smarter business. Turn large, scattered datasets into clear, actionable intelligence and make
confident, data-driven decisions across every department.

Data That Speaks Business
-------------------------

Unify information from across your organization into real-time dashboards. Cyllo transforms raw numbers
into revenue forecasts and business insight, so the story behind your data is always easy to read.

Instant Chart Builder
---------------------

Pick a model, drag your fields onto the axes, and watch Cyllo generate interactive charts automatically.
No code, no complexity, just fast visual answers to your questions.

Query-Driven Visuals
--------------------

Every field selection becomes an optimized SQL query that renders an interactive chart instantly. You
get the power of hand-written queries without ever writing a line of code.

AI-Powered Insights
-------------------

Ask questions about any chart on your dashboard and receive intelligent, data-based answers on the spot.
Cyllo surfaces the trends and explanations that would otherwise stay buried in the numbers.

One Dashboard. Total Clarity.
-----------------------------

Bring your key metrics together in one place with interactive charts that update in real time. Spot
trends the moment they emerge and accelerate every decision.

Arrange. Adjust. Analyze.
-------------------------

Personalize dashboard layouts to match how you work, and embed insights directly into other modules
for instant access. Deep analysis, growth forecasting, real-time sync, and strong security come built in.
