Cyllo Project
-------------

The Cyllo <a href="https://www.cyllo.com/project">Project Management</a> app helps you
plan and deliver projects seamlessly. Organize tasks, visualize timelines with Gantt
charts, track time, allocate resources and collaborate, all connected to your ERP.

Turn plans into progress with automated workflows that combine task management,
integrated time tracking, centralized collaboration and automatic progress reporting.

Turning Plans Into Progress
---------------------------

Project delivery needs structure. Cyllo automates your workflows by pairing task
management with built-in time tracking, centralizing collaboration and generating
progress reports so the whole team stays aligned and informed.

Your Portfolio, Visually Organized
----------------------------------

The Kanban dashboard displays every project as a card, giving you a clear, organized
overview of your ongoing initiatives, all visible at a glance.

Step Inside the Project Workspace
---------------------------------

Open any project to see its tasks, team members, deadlines and activities in one
organized space, designed to keep execution clear and collaboration effortless.

Your Tasks, Organized by Time
-----------------------------

Tasks are grouped into Inbox, Today, This Week and This Month, making it simple to
plan around time and prioritize the work that matters most right now.

Track Time, Measure Work
------------------------

Log the time spent on tasks and projects with accuracy, helping teams monitor effort,
productivity and billable hours in one place. Set milestones to track progress toward
major deliverables and keep everyone moving in the same direction.

From Task Data to Clear Insights
--------------------------------

Access completion rates, deadlines and effort data to measure performance and inform
decisions. Flexible pivot analysis lets you group and filter project data in real time
to surface trends and key performance metrics.
