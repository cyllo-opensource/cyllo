Cyllo Inventory
---------------

The Cyllo <a href="https://www.cyllo.com/inventory">Inventory Management</a> app gives you smart stock
and a smarter business. Track goods in real time across multiple warehouses and locations, from the
moment they arrive until they are delivered, with complete visibility over your entire supply chain.

Smart Stock. Smarter Business.
------------------------------

Combine stock control, warehouse operations, batch tracking, and replenishment planning in a single
unified system. Cyllo keeps your inventory levels accurate and every movement visible, so you always
know exactly what you have and where it is.

Know What Arrived, and Where It Goes Next
-----------------------------------------

Receive incoming goods through a simple, guided receipt flow that records, checks, and places every
item in the right location. Nothing gets lost between the loading dock and the shelf.

Pick, Pack, Ship Without Guesswork
----------------------------------

Turn warehouse activity into a smooth, predictable process from pick to delivery. Stock is pre-verified
and reserved in advance, so your team ships with confidence and no last-minute surprises.

Work Smarter with Batch Transfers
---------------------------------

Group multiple stock transfers into a single batch for faster, more organized fulfillment. Wave-based
picking lets your warehouse handle more orders in less time.

Real-Time Inventory Adjustments
-------------------------------

Correct physical count discrepancies the instant they appear, and let automatic reordering rules keep
you ahead of stockouts. Your numbers stay trustworthy and your shelves stay full.

Know Your Stock, Instantly
--------------------------

Visual summaries put current inventory at your fingertips, backed by stock history, valuation, and
aging analysis. Make informed decisions with barcode scanning, flexible routes, and full traceability.
