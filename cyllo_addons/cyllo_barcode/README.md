Cyllo Barcode
-------------

The Cyllo <a href="https://www.cyllo.com/barcode">Barcode</a> app brings fast, accurate
tracking to your warehouse through barcode generation, label printing, scanning and
real-time inventory management.

Activate barcode-driven workflows to eliminate manual logging, keep stock levels
optimal as you scale, and turn every scan into an instant operational action.

Scan Fast, Process Faster
-------------------------

Integrate barcode functionality directly into your warehouse operations. Every scan
triggers immediate updates across your automated workflows, so manual logging
disappears and stock levels stay accurate as your business grows.

Inventory in Motion
-------------------

Turn barcode scans into instant warehouse actions. Handle transfers, location checks,
document access and receipt or delivery management from a single unified dashboard,
without ever leaving the flow of work.

Run Your Warehouse at the Speed of a Scan
-----------------------------------------

From receiving through to fulfilment, teams can track statuses and trigger actions
instantly. The result is greater precision, fewer errors and more intuitive inventory
handling at every stage.

Incoming Transfers at a Glance
------------------------------

Pending receipts appear in a clean, card-based layout with partner details and
timestamps. Process incoming transfers quickly with single-click access to exactly
what needs attention.

Validate Items Instantly
------------------------

Scan a barcode or confirm manually to validate products, quantities and locations.
You stay in full control of every operation while keeping the pace of the warehouse
high.

What the Barcode Module Brings
------------------------------

Instantly connect scanned barcodes to product records, update inventory in real time
as stock moves, and organize work efficiently with built-in batch and wave management.
