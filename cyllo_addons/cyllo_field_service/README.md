Cyllo Field Service
-------------------

The Cyllo <a href="https://www.cyllo.com/field-service">Field Service</a> app is a
comprehensive solution for managing field service requests. Track requests through their
full lifecycle, assign the right people based on their skills, complete work checklists,
and invoice the customer, all from one place.

Service Request Lifecycle
-------------------------

Manage every request through a clear set of stages, Draft, Submitted, Assigned, In
Progress, Completed and Cancelled, with automatic sequence numbering, priority levels,
submission, assignment and deadline dates, and a Kanban view that expands every stage.

Skill Categories and Checklists
-------------------------------

Group the skills needed for a job into reusable skill categories, and attach preset service
checklists of service products with their cost and required time so that selecting a
category populates the request's task list automatically.

Skill-Based Worker Assignment
-----------------------------

Fetch suitable workers by matching request skills against employee skills, review them in a
suggestion wizard ordered by availability, and add them individually or in bulk. Each
employee's availability is computed automatically as Available, Reserved or Not Available.

Checklist Completion and Invoicing
----------------------------------

Mark checklist items as done once the service is in progress, then generate customer
invoices from the completed items or from timesheet hours, with optional linkage to a
related sale order for seamless billing.

Deadline Reminders
------------------

A scheduled action sends reminder emails to assigned workers a configurable number of days
before a request's deadline, using a dedicated mail template so field teams never miss an
approaching due date.

Customer Portal
---------------

Customers can submit and follow up on their field service requests from the Cyllo portal,
with each request exposing its own secure access URL through the portal mixin.

Reporting
---------

Produce field service reports in both PDF and Excel formats, filtered by customer, state or
sale order and grouped by state, priority, company, skill category or sale order, over any
chosen date range.
