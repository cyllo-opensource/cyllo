Cyllo AI
--------

The Cyllo <a href="https://www.cyllo.com/cyllo-ai">AI</a> app unlocks intelligent business operations with an
AI-powered ERP, delivering intelligent automation, instant insights, predictive analytics and smarter
decision-making.

AI That Works For You
---------------------

Cyllo AI transforms your data into actionable insights, acting as a proactive digital assistant that handles core
AI help and full CRUD operations. Manage records in natural language and turn your ERP into a self-driving
workspace.

Talk to Your Data
-----------------

Use plain natural language queries to pull business insights without building a single manual report. Ask a
question and get immediate, precise answers.

Your Smart Analytics Agent
--------------------------

The platform continuously examines your business data, providing clear answers and actionable intelligence
whenever you need it.

Ask, Analyze, Visualize
-----------------------

Every query returns both detailed answers and visual charts. Explore your results across multiple chart formats
for a complete, at-a-glance understanding of your business.

Do More Than Analyze
--------------------

Go beyond insights to create, update and manage business records through natural language. Every action Cyllo AI
proposes is presented for your approval before it is carried out.

Powerful Cyllo AI Capabilities
------------------------------

Multi-turn conversations, command execution across your systems and validated execution with user approval come
together to make Cyllo AI a trustworthy partner in daily operations.
