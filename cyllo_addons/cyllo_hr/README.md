Cyllo Employees
---------------

The Cyllo <a href="https://www.cyllo.com/employees">Employee Management</a> app keeps
your workforce data organized in one place, centralizing employee records, contracts,
documents, onboarding, performance reviews and organizational structure.

Bring secure, structured HR data together so your teams can grow sustainably while
minimizing compliance risks and administrative delays.

Secure, Centralized Workforce Data
----------------------------------

A well-structured internal organization is essential for sustainable growth. Cyllo
helps you oversee team roles and departmental changes from a single system, reducing
compliance risk and cutting administrative overhead.

A Smart, Visual Directory
-------------------------

A clean Kanban layout displays employee profile photos, roles and contact details with
quick actions, giving you clear visibility across the entire company at a glance.

Centralized Data and Real-Time Insights
----------------------------------------

Access insights on workforce demographics, performance and training needs securely
from one location, so decisions are always backed by up-to-date information.

Your Company, Illustrated Through Its People
--------------------------------------------

The organizational chart turns hierarchy into a visual narrative, showing how managers,
teams and individual contributors come together to build your organization. Each
profile also carries its own mini-hierarchy of reporting relationships and cross-team
connections.

A Structured Map of Skills
--------------------------

Skills are organized by type to display capability areas and pinpoint where your talent
excels, giving you a clear picture of the strengths across your teams.

A Canvas of Skills and Progress
--------------------------------

A pivot view charts skill progression as a growth map across languages, technical
expertise and development areas, so you can see potential and plan development with
confidence.
