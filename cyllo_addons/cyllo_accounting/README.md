Cyllo Accounting
----------------

The Cyllo <a href="https://www.cyllo.com/accounting">Accounting</a> app delivers a complete
solution for bookkeeping, bank reconciliation, and automated tax compliance, giving
businesses a smarter way to manage their finances end to end.

Automate accounting and gain real-time cash flow visibility through bank synchronization,
tightly integrated with your sales and inventory systems.

Your Financial Command Center
-----------------------------

The dashboard gives you instant visibility into invoices, payments, balances, and cash
flow. Track key metrics and analyze trends using always up-to-date financial information,
so every decision rests on confident, accurate numbers.

Match. Reconcile. Done.
-----------------------

Match payments, invoices, and bank transactions in just a few clicks. Cyllo automatically
detects matches and suggests entries, making reconciliation faster and error-free. Validate
bank lines through an intuitive interface to keep your books perfectly balanced.

A Clear Window to Every Payment
-------------------------------

Manage approvals, validations, and follow-ups from consolidated payment lists. Handle
refunds and credit notes directly within the payment form, so every transaction stays
transparent and fully accounted for.

See the Numbers Behind Every Entry
----------------------------------

Examine debit and credit entries at a granular level to reveal exactly how transactions
flow through your accounts. Detailed journal records show the accounting impact of each
entry with full account and balance clarity.

Reports That Tell the Whole Story
---------------------------------

Get balance sheets, profit and loss statements, and general ledger reports that present
your assets, liabilities, and equity in a single view. See the financial position and
stability of your business at a glance.

Tools That Power Your Finances
------------------------------

From Anglo-Saxon accounting, deferred revenue and expenses, and multi-currency support to
fiscal positions, budget management, tax returns, post-dated cheques, and analytic
accounting, Cyllo gives you every tool your finance team needs in one place.
