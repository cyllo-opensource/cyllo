Cyllo Purchase
--------------

The Cyllo <a href="https://www.cyllo.com/purchase">Purchase</a> app optimizes procurement
from end to end, helping you manage vendors, RFQs, and automated purchase orders while
gaining the insights you need to streamline buying.

Smart Buying Starts Here
------------------------

The Purchase module brings vendor management, requests for quotation, purchase orders,
stock updates, and billing together in one unified system, giving you controlled spending
and full visibility over every transaction.

See Your Buying Journey in Motion
---------------------------------

View all your RFQs and purchase orders in one organized list, complete with vendor details,
status, amounts, and next actions. At-a-glance dashboard panels summarize key metrics like
RFQ count, average value, and lead time for faster, informed decisions.

Where Every Request Begins Its Journey
--------------------------------------

Create and review quotation requests with vendor details, product lines, quantities, and
pricing through an intuitive form. Every purchase starts from a clear, structured request
that keeps procurement on track.

Compare Vendors. Choose the Best Offer.
---------------------------------------

Compare pricing, terms, and delivery conditions side by side to make cost-effective
purchasing selections. Cyllo helps you choose the best offer every time, so your budget
goes further.

Track Spend. Understand Trends.
-------------------------------

Monitor purchasing volumes, costs, and supplier activity with real-time visualizations.
Detailed purchase reports evaluate supplier performance, product costs, and order
efficiency to support data-driven procurement decisions.

Core Purchase Capabilities
--------------------------

With procurement rules, blanket orders, and vendor pricelists, Cyllo Purchase gives you a
systematic approach to buying that scales with your business.
