Cyllo Appointments
------------------

The Cyllo <a href="https://www.cyllo.com/appointment">Appointments</a> app is a comprehensive
scheduling solution that lets you publish bookable services, manage the staff and resources that
deliver them, and keep customers informed through automated multi-channel notifications.

Appointment Types & Categories
------------------------------

Define reusable appointment types classified as consultations, services, meetings, classes/training,
events or other. Each type carries its own duration, capacity, location, working hours and booking
policies, and can be published to the website for public booking.

Resource & Staff Management
---------------------------

Manage bookable resources such as rooms, equipment, vehicles and tools, each with its own capacity
and optional working-hours calendar, and assign staff members drawn from your employees. Types can
require a resource or a staff assignment, and slots respect the availability of both.

Flexible Scheduling & Slots
---------------------------

Choose between dynamic scheduling based on working hours or predefined time slots, with configurable
slot intervals, minimum booking notice and a maximum booking horizon. Slots track their booked count
against the maximum attendees allowed per slot.

Multi-Channel Notifications & Reminders
---------------------------------------

Automatically send confirmations, reminders, follow-ups and cancellation notices over email, SMS and
WhatsApp. Reminders support multiple lead times, and a scheduled action dispatches them at the right
moment before each appointment.

Online Booking & Customer Portal
--------------------------------

Customers can book appointments directly from the website and manage them from the portal, where they
can reschedule or cancel within the deadlines you configure. The full appointment lifecycle covers
confirmation, start, completion, cancellation and no-show handling.

Paid Appointments & Refunds
---------------------------

Attach a service product to charge for appointments and collect payment online before confirmation.
Configurable refund policies and time-based refund tiers determine the refund percentage on
cancellation, with automatic credit notes and refund payments to reconcile the transaction.
