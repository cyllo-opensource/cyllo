Cyllo Help Desk
---------------

The Cyllo <a href="https://www.cyllo.com/help-desk">Help Desk</a> app provides a complete platform to receive, assign
and resolve customer support tickets. Manage teams, service-level agreements, reporting, dashboards, customer ratings
and daily targets from a single, organized workspace.

Ticket Management
----------------

Log support tickets with an auto-generated reference, priority, customer details, category, tags and rich descriptions,
and keep private context in dedicated internal notes visible only to staff. Tickets flow through configurable stages
with a full stage history that records how long each ticket spends in every stage.

Teams and Assignment
-------------------

Organize agents into helpdesk teams with email aliases, working-hours calendars and live counters for open, unassigned,
urgent and SLA-failed tickets. Users can be tagged with helpdesk skills to route work to the right people, and a team
success rate highlights overall performance.

Service-Level Agreements
-----------------------

Define SLA policies that target a resolution stage within a set number of working hours, scoped by team, category, tag
or customer, and exclude selected stages from the countdown. SLA statuses are tracked per ticket and refreshed
automatically by a scheduled action so deadlines, passes and failures stay current.

Customer Portal and Live Chat
----------------------------

Let customers submit and follow their tickets from the self-service portal, and automatically create tickets from live
chat conversations, capturing the visitor and the full conversation history as the ticket description.

Canned Responses and Ratings
---------------------------

Speed up replies with reusable canned responses, and collect customer feedback through rating requests that feed a
dedicated rating analysis by ticket, team, assigned user and stage.

Daily Targets and Overview
-------------------------

Set per-user daily targets for ticket count, success rate and average rating, and track achievement against them on a
personal overview dashboard that summarizes each agent's workload and performance.

Merging, Returns and Reporting
-----------------------------

Merge duplicate tickets into a single destination while consolidating their messages, attachments and dependencies,
initiate stock return flows linked to a ticket, and generate detailed PDF and Excel reports filtered by date, category
and customer alongside built-in pivot and graph analysis.
