Cyllo eCommerce
---------------

The Cyllo <a href="https://www.cyllo.com/ecommerce">eCommerce</a> platform is a modern way to
start selling online, managing products, carts, payments, shipping and inventory from one
place so your website becomes a true shopping destination.

Sell Beyond Store Walls
-----------------------

Transform your website into a shopping destination with secure payments and real-time
inventory syncing. Order processing is automated, turning first-time visitors into loyal,
returning customers.

Configure Products the Way You Sell
-----------------------------------

Set up products with variants, pricing and images in minutes. Customize options and track
stock in real time so your catalog always reflects what is actually available.

A Checkout That Feels Effortless
--------------------------------

Streamline the journey from cart to payment to reduce abandonment and boost conversions.
Support multiple payment methods so every customer can pay the way they prefer.

Payments You Can Trust
----------------------

Process every transaction securely with built-in protections and transparent integration,
from order confirmation all the way through to completion.

All Your Orders, Perfectly Organized
------------------------------------

View sales orders in an easy-to-navigate list with statuses, dates and totals. Drill into
any order to see detailed line items and pricing, keeping customers informed at every step.

Tools Built for Seamless Selling
--------------------------------

Synchronize website sales with your backend systems, manage pricing and inventory
dynamically, and integrate multiple payment gateways, all from one unified dashboard.
