Cyllo Sales
-----------

The Cyllo <a href="https://www.cyllo.com/sales">Sales</a> app delivers comprehensive and
effortless sales management, with advanced lead tracking, pipeline management, quotation
automation, and seamless CRM integration to accelerate every deal you close.

Unify lead management, quotations, order processing, CRM, and invoicing into a single
platform that gives you complete visibility across the sales cycle.

Better Sales, Less Effort
-------------------------

Move from quotation to sales order to invoice without the friction. Modern quotation
formats and automated workflows generate invoices upon delivery, so the path from proposal
to payment stays smooth and clear.

Browse. Click. Shop.
--------------------

An online store integrates directly with your sales and inventory systems for instant,
accurate order processing. Secure payments, auto-filled details, and real-time order
confirmation make checkout smooth for every customer.

Your Order, Clearly Presented
-----------------------------

Get an organized view of each sales order, complete with items, pricing, taxes, and
delivery status. Everything customers need lives in one accessible portal that centralizes
their orders, invoices, and support interactions.

Insights That Drive Decisions
-----------------------------

Real-time reports track revenue, orders, and growth trends so you can measure progress at a
glance. Analyze sales patterns and compare performance to uncover new business
opportunities.

Everything You Need to Sell Smarter
-----------------------------------

From pricelists, discounts, and a rich product catalog to upsell and cross-sell tools,
sales team management, and shipping cost calculation, Cyllo Sales gives you every feature
you need to sell smarter.
