Cyllo Green Metrics
-------------------

The Cyllo <a href="https://www.cyllo.com/green-metrics">Green Metrics</a> app helps organizations measure and manage
their environmental footprint by tracking carbon, water and sound emissions. Configure emission sources, factors and
assignment rules to produce accurate, consistent and automated greenhouse gas calculations across the company.

Emission Sources and Scopes
---------------------------

Define emission sources categorized as energy, transport, materials, waste, services or other, and organize them into
emission scopes (such as Scope 1, Scope 2 and Scope 3) with company-specific codes for structured reporting.

Emission Factors
----------------

Maintain air, sound and water emission factors with values, units, validity date ranges and greenhouse gas links.
Each factor can store IPCC EFDB references, IPCC category codes and a Global Warming Potential reference for
standards-aligned calculations.

Greenhouse Gases
---------------

Record the greenhouse gases behind your emissions with unique codes, chemical formulas and Global Warming Potential
values, so emissions can be expressed and compared in CO2-equivalent terms.

Activities and Calculations
---------------------------

Capture individual emission activities with a quantity, source and factor, then group them into calculation records
that roll up total air, water and sound emissions, identify the highest emitting source and move through draft,
submitted, approved and done stages.

Automated Assignment Rules
--------------------------

Set up priority-ordered assignment rules that match activity records against a domain and automatically apply the
appropriate emission source and factor, or compute emissions directly, removing manual data entry. A dedicated compute
wizard applies these rules, calculates emissions and closes the calculation in a single step.

Fleet Integration
----------------

Integrate with the Cyllo Fleet app to derive per-vehicle CO2 and sound emission factors from vehicle and model data,
with a scheduled action that keeps vehicle-based emission factors up to date.

Interactive Dashboard
--------------------

Visualize sustainability performance through a dedicated dashboard with charts for emissions by gas and by scope,
air, sound and water trends, and progress against carbon and water caps and targets defined at the company level.

Carbon Credit Transfers
----------------------

Manage carbon credit transfers through dedicated credit products and invoices, letting you record and offset
emissions against tradable carbon credits within your accounting flow.
