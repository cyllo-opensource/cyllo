Cyllo Point of Sale
-------------------

The Cyllo <a href="https://www.cyllo.com/pos">Point of Sale</a> app is built for modern retail and
restaurants. It keeps your lines moving with fast billing, works online and offline, and stays connected
to your inventory and accounting data in real time.

Fast Billing. Better Business.
------------------------------

A robust point of sale keeps checkout quick and customers happy. Cyllo processes every sale in seconds
through a streamlined flow, minimizing wait times and lifting the quality of your service.

Fast Checkout, From Counter to Customer
---------------------------------------

An intuitive interface guides each transaction through item selection, order review, and payment in as
few steps as possible. New staff get up to speed fast, and every checkout stays smooth.

A Clear Record of Every Transaction
-----------------------------------

Track POS orders end to end to monitor sales history, resolve customer concerns, and keep a verifiable
audit trail. Payments are linked to their orders and sessions for complete traceability.

Take Charge of Every POS Session
--------------------------------

Get full visibility into shift openings, sales activity, and closing balance reconciliation. Cash
management tools make it easy to keep every session accountable from start to finish.

Design How Your Customers Pay
-----------------------------

Configure the payment methods that fit your business, from cash and cards to digital wallets and payment
terminals. Integrated devices connect seamlessly for a frictionless checkout.

Where Every Order Stays on Track
--------------------------------

A real-time kitchen display organizes incoming orders by preparation status for coordinated service,
while analytics and session reporting reveal performance trends across every location and timeframe.
